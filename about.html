Our story

One key,
every city.

Shelton Hotels grew from the TM Group's decades in Pakistan's hospitality business. What began as a handful of guest houses in Peshawar's University Town has grown, city by city, into a network spanning the country.

How it began

From Peshawar to Lahore — and everywhere between

From the pine valleys of Kumrat and Swat to the boulevards of Lahore, every Shelton property carries one identity: the Shelton key mark. It's a small signal of a bigger promise — that whichever city you land in, someone at the front desk already knows you're coming.

Today, Shelton operates across Peshawar, Islamabad, Lahore, Rawalpindi, Swat, Dir, Mardan, Bannu, Dera Ismail Khan, and Buner — with more on the horizon.

"Every branch answers its own phone. That's not an accident — it's the whole model."
1990s

Guest houses in Peshawar

The TM Group's first hospitality properties established in University Town, Peshawar — building the foundation of what would become Shelton Hotels.

2000s

Expansion into KPK

Growth into additional cities across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with new branches in Mardan, Swat, and the Dir region serving both business and leisure travellers.

2010s

Islamabad & Punjab

Shelton Hotels expands into the Federal Capital and Punjab, opening properties in Islamabad's key sectors, Rawalpindi, and Lahore's commercial districts.

Today

24 properties, 10 cities

A unified national brand with 24 properties across 10 cities — and a commitment to direct, personal hospitality at every single one.
